Our Club

A Club For The Community

Walsall Aces Tennis Club is about more than simply playing tennis.

We're building a home for people who both enjoy watching and playing the game. If you want to play for fun or competetively, improve your skills and connect with others doing the same, come along and join us at WATC.

We mainly play at Walsall Arboretum but we also often organise sessions at the other tennis courts located across the borough.

WATC

Our Vison

Exceptional tennis. Right here in our local park. We want Walsall Aces to become the kind of tennis community that makes people stop and watch. Not because we're trying to be the biggest club, or because winning is everything. But because the tennis is **fast, skilful, intense, athletic and exciting** — and because the people playing it genuinely love what they're doing. We want to create an environment where good players become better, where newcomers discover what they're capable of, and where people who might never otherwise have met find each other through a shared love of tennis, fitness and healthy living. Our ambition is simple: **Raise the standard of tennis without losing the spirit that brought us together.** We want someone walking through the park to see the Aces playing and think: “Wow, those guys are awesome. I wonder if they would teach me to be too.” That's the Walsall Aces we are striving to build.
