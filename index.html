Welcome to Walsall Aces

More than just tennis — we're about creating a positive, welcoming environment where people can play, improve, compete and connect.

A club for the community

Walsall Aces Tennis Club is built around the idea that tennis should be accessible, enjoyable and social. Whether you're picking up a racket for the first time or looking to develop your competitive game, there's space for you here.

Our aim is to create opportunities for players of different ages and abilities while building a strong tennis community in Walsall.

Tennis for everyone

Whether you're learning the basics, developing your technique or enjoying competitive tennis, Walsall Aces is about helping players get more from the game.

🎾

Learn

Develop your skills, build confidence and enjoy learning the fundamentals of tennis in a supportive environment.

🏆

Compete

Challenge yourself, play competitive tennis and enjoy the atmosphere that comes from testing your game.

🤝

Connect

Meet new people, make friends and become part of a tennis community built around participation.
