How we use your information

Walsall Aces Tennis Club collects the information provided on this form to administer club membership, maintain appropriate membership records and support club administration and insurance requirements.

We aim to collect only information that is relevant and necessary for these purposes.

The club will normally rely on an appropriate lawful basis under UK data-protection law for membership administration. Where the club relies on legitimate interests, those interests include the administration and operation of the tennis club, maintaining appropriate membership records and managing relevant insurance requirements.

Your information may be accessed by authorised people responsible for club administration and, where necessary, shared with relevant organisations such as the club's insurer where this is necessary for the stated purpose and permitted by law.

Membership information is stored in a restricted-access Google Sheet. Access is limited to authorised club personnel who need the information for club administration.

We will retain personal information only for as long as it is necessary for the purposes for which it was collected, including any applicable insurance, accounting or legal requirements. The club will review retained information periodically and delete information that is no longer required.

You may have rights under UK data-protection law, including rights to request access to your information, correction of inaccurate information, restriction of processing and, where applicable, erasure or objection.

If you have a question about how your information is handled, please contact the club through its normal contact arrangements.

Please do not use the Notes field to provide medical information, health information or other sensitive personal information.
